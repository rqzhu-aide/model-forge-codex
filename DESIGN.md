# Design System: Model Forge

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Scholars' Forge"**

Model Forge is a scholarly workshop inside a dwarven foundry: the monumental iron and stone of a great forge around the reading room of a hall of explorers. A solo researcher comes back to it after the team has worked for hours or days. The frame tells them where they are: a navy iron sidebar, a lintel over the project that holds its title and the five phases, and heat in exactly one place in each part of the frame. Inside that frame the page is a reading room. White panels on pale stone, one sans family, generous prose and crisp mathematics, with nothing moving while they read.

The character is in the architecture, not in the content. Riveted iron plates brushed to a dull sheen, stepped double rules, chamfered corners and two iron brackets sit on the sidebar, the project lintel, the record and the major titles, and every card is bound at its corners in iron with a carved course under its title. Explanations, equations, comparisons and reports inside the cards get none of it. Color still stands for ownership inside the reading room: Illini Blue for the team and the system, teal-green for system activity, ember for whatever the researcher writes, saves or launches. Illini Orange is the forge's heat and is never a role color.

Motion is a grammar of three. Control states fade over 150ms, a running phase's dot and the glowing rim of its tab breathe together every 1.6s, and the Next jump lands with a single arrival mark: a 2px Illini Blue outline that fades over 600ms on an exponential ease-out. Reading surfaces never animate. Light and dark themes are equal; the iron frame is the same navy in both, and the reading room changes from pale stone to deep slate.

This file records the system as built in `web/src/styles.css`, `web/src/technicalReportReading.css` and the shared components in `web/src/components/Visuals.jsx`. Per-phase reading rules, the exact phase labels, discussion ownership and attention rules, and the validation record live in `design-system/model-forge/MASTER.md`; follow that file for those, not this one. Where the two disagree, the build wins.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A frame of worked navy iron (Illini Blue #13294B and its five-step ramp) in riveted, brushed plates with bevels and inset shadows, around a reading room of white panels on pale stone (#eeebe5).
- Illini Orange (#FF5F05) as heat, used one place at a time: the active project, the current phase, the Next action, the selected method, the lintel's glow line and the crucible in the workbench drawing.
- Marcellus inscriptional capitals for the Model Forge name and a few major titles; IBM Plex Sans for everything people read or press; Plex Mono for code, identifiers and figures. All three are bundled.
- Ornament on the frame and the edges of cards only: sidebar, lintel, record, report titles, artwork, card corner fittings and the carved course under card titles. Card bodies stay plain, untextured and still.
- The record (phase history and the technical report's title) has the parchment and double rules of a library.
- Ownership is a small dot and a word on the card's title; every state has a word next to it.

## Colors

The frame is a navy ramp lit from within by one orange; the reading room is warm pale stone, white panels and hairlines. Three role hues mark ownership inside the reading room, and amber and red only qualify text.

### The forge (frame)
- **Forge ramp** (#0b1a31, #0f2140, **Illini Blue #13294B**, #1b355d, #24426f, #3a5786): the sidebar and the project lintel, as top-to-bottom gradients from forge-3 through forge-2 to forge-1, with forge-0 used at reduced opacity for recesses (the active project slot, the theme track, the activity strip, the stepper's state wells). ### Named Rules
**The One Heat Rule.** Illini Orange is the forge's fire and appears in one place per region of the frame: the active project's chevron, the current phase's tab, the Next action, the selected method's lozenge, the lintel's glow line and the crucible in the workbench drawing. It never fills a reading surface, never colors body text and never marks a role.

**The Iron Frame Rule.** The navy ramp, riveted plating and brackets belong to the frame: the sidebar, the project lintel and the artwork. Inside the reading room navy is only ink, a primary button, a selected tab or the iron fittings at a card's corners.

**The Warm Hands Rule.** Warm peach, cream and ember belong to the researcher: what they type, what they saved and actions taken for them. A control does not inherit the ownership of the prose around it; Cancel inside a researcher editor stays a system control.

**The Dot, Not Tint Rule.** A card's role is a 6px dot before its title and a 12px muted label after it (Team, Your input, Activity, Record, Revision). Card bodies stay white and heading bands stay neutral; the violet revision summary and the parchment history record are the only surfaces tinted whole.

**The Beside-Text Rule.** Amber and red appear only next to words that say the same thing. If you removed every color, every state would still read.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Marcellus 400 (with Georgia, Times New Roman, serif)
**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans Variable (with IBM Plex Sans, Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono 400 and 500 (with Cascadia Mono, Consolas, monospace)

**Character:** Marcellus is an inscriptional face with flared, chiselled capitals, the voice of names cut into stone. It is used sparingly for the identity and for a few titles a researcher orients by. Everything they read, compare or press is IBM Plex Sans, an engineered humanist sans; Plex Mono is its sibling for code, identifiers and figures. All three are bundled through @fontsource and served with the app.

### Hierarchy
- **Arrival title** (Marcellus 400, 30px, 1.2, .01em): the "Begin with your problem" heading under the crest.
- **Display title** (Marcellus 400, 26px, 1.25, .01em): the project title on the lintel (21px below 600px), other page titles, the technical report's title and its level-one section headings.
- **Brand** (Marcellus 400, 20px, 1.15, .03em): "Model Forge" beside the mark (18px below 800px).
- **Record title** (Marcellus 400, 17px, 1.35, .01em): the summary line of a history record.
- **Report heading** (Plex 600, 18px, 1.4): lower section headings inside the technical report, in Team Ink.
- **Title** (Plex 600, 17px, 1.4, -.015em): one heading per card in its band, document window titles and a selected method's title.
- **Subsection** (Plex 600, 16px, 1.45, -.015em): h3 inside cards and documents. Markdown h4 and h5 are 16px at 600; h6 is the same in muted ink.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.65): scientific prose, held to a 36em measure (about 72 characters). Equations, tables, plots and code may use the full card width.
- **Contribution** (400, 16px, 1.55): researcher writing in editable fields and saved contributions.
- **UI body** (400, 15px, 1.55): the page's base text.
- **Data** (400, 14px, 1.55, tabular figures): table cells. Table headers are 12px, sentence case, on Band Stone.
- **Label** (600, 13px, 20px): buttons, tabs and field labels.
- **Meta** (500, 13px, 20px): source links, metadata and the setting link.
- **Field** (400, 13px, 20px): field text and sidebar items; fields rise to 16px below 600px so phones do not zoom.
- **Stepper** (500, 12.5px, 1.3): phase names on the tab plates in Forge Text; the current phase is 650 in forge-0 on the fire. Status cues are 600, 12px, tabular.
- **Caption** (500, 12px, 1.3): role labels, the brand subtitle and the sidebar legend. Meaningful status never goes below this size.
- **Mono** (400, 0.92em): code, identifiers and ids, with tabular figures.

### Named Rules
**The Inscription Rule.** Marcellus is cut only where a researcher orients: the brand, the project and page titles, the arrival title, history records and the report's title and top sections. It never sets body text, controls, tables, labels or mathematics, and it is only ever weight 400.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Labels, table headers and sidebar sections are sentence case. Only table headers keep a slight tracking (.025em); Plex headings tighten instead, and Marcellus titles open slightly (.01em to .03em). Plex weights stop at 400, 500 and 600.

**The Figures Line Up Rule.** Counts, ids, table cells, stepper numerals and report metadata use tabular figures.

## Layout

The shell is a 208px iron sidebar and a fluid main column capped at 1400px, padded 16px 20px 32px. Reading runs as full-width sections stacked vertically, 16px apart; there is no dashboard grid.

At the top of a project, the lintel hangs from the top edge of the main column, 20px wider than the column on each side, and stays sticky while reading. Its first row holds the Marcellus title and the activity strip; its second holds the five-step stepper. The research question follows on the stone (two lines at most, its row capped at the prose measure plus 18ch for the setting link), then the Next line, with 24px below them before the first card. At 1100px and wider, when the question row is present, the forge-hall plate sits to the right of the question and Next at 184px, level with them; otherwise it is not shown.

Spacing is a 4px scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px). Card heading bands are padded 8px 16px and bodies 14px 16px 16px.

Responsive behavior:
- Below 1200px, the theory graph and its inspector stack.
- From 601px to 1100px, phase wells stack above their full-width names; phase names wrap only between words.
- Below 800px, the sidebar becomes a flat iron band above the content behind a "Projects & settings" toggle, its edge rules and brand rule drop away, the lintel narrows to 10px overhangs with 10px bottom corners, and grids fall to one column.
- Below 600px, the lintel title drops to 21px and its brackets are hidden, card padding drops to 12px, stepper names hide, and each tab plate centres its state well and concise status. Only the current phase's full name shows beneath the plates. The question drops to 15px and the Next button takes the full width.
- At 440px and below, phase plates form three then two columns so exact status words remain readable at 12px, including when a vertical scrollbar reduces the available width.
- On coarse pointers, every control, summary and sidebar item grows to 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

The frame has depth; the reading room is flat. The sidebar and lintel are lit as worked metal: a vertical gradient from forge-3 to forge-1, riveted iron plating (staggered 96 by 48 plates, each with a dark seam, a steel bevel along its top and left edges, a shadowed bottom and right edge, a face that catches light at its upper left and a small domed rivet in each corner, brushed lengthwise with a faint grain) under a broad specular sheen (a pillar of light down the sidebar, a slanting band across the lintel), inset shadows where something is recessed and a warm radial glow where the forge lights them from below. In the reading room depth still comes only from the step between stone and white panels, 1px hairlines and neutral heading bands, and cards, buttons, tabs and bands carry no shadow.

### Named Rules
**The Still Page Rule.** Nothing in the reading flow casts a shadow, glows or carries texture; only the frame, overlays and the edges of cards (their corner fittings and the carved course under the title) do. If a boundary in the reading room needs to be clearer, use a stronger line, not elevation.

## Shapes

Corners come from three steps: 6px for controls and inner containers (buttons, tabs, fields, sidebar items, the Next line, the theme track), 8px for cards, history records and document windows, and fully round for role dots and the outcome badge. Where `corner-shape: bevel` is supported (Chromium), cards, history records, the Next line and its button, the sidebar's project items (the active slot and the raised plates), the theme switch, the activity strip, the stepper's tab plates and the lintel's 14px lower corners are chamfered instead of rounded; other browsers show the same radii rounded. The stepper's state wells are fully round, recessed into the plates. The brand tile, the crest, the forge-hall plate and the workbench niche are drawn with clipped corners. Side rules inside documents are 1px, never thicker. Disclosures share one cue: a 7px chevron drawn in 1.75px strokes that turns from right-pointing to down when open.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep reading surfaces Panel White (#ffffff; #161c25 in dark) on a 1px Card Line with a neutral Band Stone heading band and 8px corners; only the revision summary is violet and only history records are parchment.
- **Do** keep the frame (sidebar, lintel) in the forge ramp in both themes, with texture, bevels, rivets and brackets concentrated there; in the reading room iron appears only as the fittings at card corners.
- **Do** spend heat once per region: the active project, the current phase, the Next action, the selected method, the lintel's glow line and the workbench crucible. The mark's furnace and the forging mark's strike heat and sparks belong to the mark and stay inside it.
- **Do** set the brand, the project and page titles, the arrival title, history records and the report's title in Marcellus 400, and everything else in Plex.
- **Do** name a card's role with the 6px dot before its title and the label after it: Team, Your input, Activity, Record or Revision.
- **Do** put a word beside every state: stepper cues, status chips, the Next label.
- **Do** size controls at 28px with 6px corners on fine pointers and 44px on coarse pointers.
- **Do** hold reading prose to the 36em measure at 16px/1.65, and let equations, tables, plots and code use the full card width.
- **Do** keep motion to 150ms control fades, the 1.6s running dot and rim, the single 600ms arrival outline and the 5s forging mark, and replace each with a static state under reduced motion.
- **Do** keep the one forge-gate silhouette in every version of the mark, from the favicon to the crest.
- **Do** follow `design-system/model-forge/MASTER.md` for per-phase reading rules and exact phase labels.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put texture, glow, bevels or ornament on prose, equations, tables, comparisons or reports; ornament stops at a card's corners and the carved course under its title.
- **Don't** use Illini Orange for text, role markers, researcher controls, status or decorative fills; ember (#b5440d) is the researcher's color and heat is the forge's.
- **Don't** put drop shadows on cards, buttons, tabs or bands in the reading room.
- **Don't** set body text, controls, labels, tables or mathematics in Marcellus, or use uppercase tracked labels, kickers above headings or Plex weights above 600.
- **Don't** add new artwork to active research and reading areas; artwork belongs where a researcher enters, orients or pauses.
- **Don't** use emblems of real factions, militaries or movements in the ornament; the Horde note is in the angular brackets and plate joints only.
- **Don't** load remote fonts or assets; Marcellus, Plex, the artwork and every icon ship with the app as files and inline SVG, and the forging mark is drawn in code.
- **Don't** let color carry state alone.
- **Don't** give new controls or containers radii outside 6px, 8px and fully round; the lintel's 14px belongs to it alone.
