# Ask for consequential input

Ask one direct question that helps the researcher shape a consequential modeling choice or supply a missing study fact. Modeling choices are eligible even when all alternatives fit the original goal, no study fact is missing and the team recommends one. Check original instructions, applicable saved answers, literature and analysis affordable within the phase first. Ask when at least two defensible directions remain with a material difference in formulation, assumptions, model or information set, scientific use, guarantee, or practical accuracy, interpretability or computational feasibility.

Handle a missing table statistic or comparison through the existing analysis, limitation or next-work fields within phase authority. A researcher question needs unavailable study knowledge or a consequential scientific commitment; an analysis gap alone is not such a choice.

A different estimator or solver alone is insufficient. Explain the scientific commitment or material tradeoff not already settled by the agreed goal, requirements and budget; handle internal choices that preserve agreed requirements within the team. Do not offer equivalent representations, known-invalid constructions or demonstrably dominated alternatives as equally viable. An investigation with uncertain feasibility can be a real option if its uncertainty and development cost are clear. Ask once alternatives and their consequences can be explained, before substantial work commits to one. Do affordable checks first, without requiring every direction to be proved or implemented. Useful optional input can be invited while justified conditional work continues. Briefly summarize consequential team choices and reasons in existing scientific fields, not another artifact. A researcher may adopt an assumption for development without verifying its truth or the proof under it.

## Consolidate before presenting

The Lead also inspects current findings, limitations and the original goal for a consequential next choice the researcher owns, even when no member proposed it. A completed or inconclusive result may expose a material decision about scientific scope, the next development direction or use of substantial resources. Apply the same eligibility, phase authority and saved-answer checks before inviting input. Missing analysis or a routine next task alone is not a researcher choice. Do not manufacture alternatives, reopen a settled preference or ask a question merely because a phase ended. In P5 this check remains limited to indispensable report-specific editorial choices; upstream scientific directions stay in the existing limitations and next-work account.

The Lead compares member proposals with each other, open questions and applicable saved answers. Use one canonical inquiry when the same answer settles both without reinterpretation under the same target, population, conditions, timing and scope. Integrate necessary distinct consequences, link affected components to the single answer location and retain original sources, history and answer applicability. Consolidation alone does not resolve an item or change pause/readiness state.

Similar wording is insufficient: what a cost measures and which cost constraint is intended are separate inputs. Preserve independently answerable modeling choices and facts; do not force bundled choices. A merged inquiry must retain both theoretical and computational consequences when specialists raise the same fork. Explain dependencies and defer a follow-up that only matters under an unchosen direction. Reuse settled applicable answers; a material premise change needs an explicit successor question explaining the new conflict. Zero new inquiries is valid. Do not split one decision into overlapping questions to shorten it.

## Compare the alternatives fairly

State what stays fixed when it matters: target, risk criterion, population, horizon, units and settled answers. In the question context, options and recommendation, name the actual target, objective, constraint or measured tradeoff behind general words such as accuracy or cost. Preserve its definition and distinguish a proposed evaluation from an observed result; do not invent a measure or an advantage to rank alternatives. Vary the named modeling choice or disclose other coupled changes. Check whether options are equivalent under conditions, nested, relaxations, conservative approximations or different requirements. Keep an implication or qualification visible when omitting it would mislead; link the derivation. Do not silently change a risk preference or reuse thresholds as if differently scaled events were equivalent.

Use neutral descriptive labels and comparable depth. Give each option's mathematical change, meaning for the supplied study, and decisive benefit and limitation. State distinctive assumptions and excluded cases. A recommendation may rest on tractability or provability if it discloses the scientific restriction or loss of flexibility. Do not present unverified advantages as established. Use actual supplied units and intended use, not invented clinical or biological meanings.

Use plain contextual labels that name what the researcher would choose. Explain what each direction would change or help them learn, its practical implications and its decisive uncertainty. Keep numerical precision proportionate to the comparison and uncertainty; exact values stay in the linked records and tables.

If an option requires revising the method, say so in its consequence, where the researcher chooses it. In P2 this means a later P2 method-revision run, or P1 work if the shared research setting changes; in later phases identify the appropriate earlier development work. A saved answer does not launch that work.

For example, per-decision bounds $\Pr_\pi(C_t>c_t)\le\alpha_t$ and a cumulative bound $\Pr_\pi(\sum_{t<T}\gamma^t C_t>b)\le\alpha$ generally protect different events. For a finite horizon and positive weights, the former imply a bound of $\min(1,\sum_{t<T}\alpha_t)$ at cumulative threshold $\sum_{t<T}\gamma^t c_t$ by the union bound, without independence. They need not imply the requested bound at arbitrary $b,\alpha$. Discounting costs also changes what protection means; do not inherit a reward discount silently. This illustrates comparison discipline, not a question to reopen when the project has already settled it.

## Reading ceilings for new inquiries

| Authored primary prose | Maximum words |
| --- | ---: |
| Direct question | 20 |
| Shared context plus overall consequence | 45 |
| Each option, including label and consequence | 30 |
| Lead recommendation/reason | 20 |
| Entire choice inquiry | 180 |
| Entire study-fact inquiry | 80 |

These are ceilings, not targets. Count all authored primary fields together, including adjacent definitions or headings. Exclude system controls, exact source identifiers and researcher responses. Compact formulas are additional; their prose explanations count, including prose inside formulas. Use a shared expression and one per mathematically distinct alternative when sufficient. Do not hide long prose in equations. Rewrite overlong inquiries while preserving essential conditions; never truncate saved text or hide decisive consequences. Full scientific explanations, requested discussion, user responses and historical records have no inquiry word cap.

Within the context budget, retain essential definitions and decisive conditions first. Cut source history, repeated context and optionality labels supplied by the UI before scientific content; move additional mechanism detail to linked sources. Authors explain what work can proceed or which action needs input; actual state supplies the UI's optional or waiting label.

## Write the smallest complete request

`question` gives the direct request. For a choice, name the scientific alternatives in the question itself in plain study terms, so the researcher can see what is being decided before expanding it; place the triggering finding nearby when relevant. Write one connected "Why this matters" account across `context_markdown` and `likely_consequence`: identify the exact scientific choice or missing fact, explain its necessary mathematical and domain meaning, then state the practical effect and decisive uncertainty once. Start `context_markdown` with the affected target/component or missing fact, defining shared symbols once; `likely_consequence` completes that account with what the answer changes, without restarting the explanation or repeating options. Do not repeat this account in `expected_consequence`, `why_it_matters` or another supporting field. Explain mathematical changes and their scientific meaning in the supplied setting; use no artificial equations for a simple fact or nonmathematical choice.

For a genuine `choice`, author usually two or three useful alternatives as unique stable `{id,label,consequence}` objects when a finite set helps. Each consequence states what distinguishes that option: the changed formulation when mathematical, its scientific meaning, its practical effect and material benefit/cost. Keep shared rationale in the common account; each option explains its own consequential tradeoff without repeating it. Every choice supplies `recommendation`: the short reason for a justified suggestion, displayed beside that option, or briefly why no ranking is justified. When justified by the stated goal and evidence, put the suggested option first and set `recommended_option_id` to its exact ID; otherwise omit the pointer. Give the reason once, without retelling the common account or copying the option's consequence. Advice is neither preselection nor a saved preference. Never infer the researcher's preference from order or require agreement. The system supplies custom-response and unknown controls; do not duplicate them as scientific options. Preserve published option order and IDs; do not republish an unchanged question merely to add a marker or reorder it.

For `study_fact`, request the actual information and why it matters. Use free text/unknown without authored options, `recommended_option_id` or recommendation. Accept partial, unknown and unavailable answers. Distinguish missing measurements from missing outcomes. Never infer which variables are unavailable from participation or assay-completion status alone; keep consequential observation-process assumptions conditional unless supplied sources establish them. Do not recommend a guessed measurement meaning or collection mechanism, or repeatedly ask for unavailable information while justified work continues.

## Discussion, answers and actual state

Targeted discussion answers the selected question from relevant sources, the saved answer and any explicitly labeled unsaved draft. Advice compares choices or identifies needed information; it does not save an answer, change published options, revise a method or launch work. Additional alternatives use the existing custom-response path. Changed question meaning uses successor/version handling.

Use the existing answer location and exact saved scope/source applicability; do not repeat an answered inquiry elsewhere. Keep selected, custom, unknown and unsaved responses distinct. Clarify consequential ambiguity in a custom request without treating it as feasible or silently mapping it to a listed option. Started work keeps its recorded inputs unless an existing continuation mechanism applies. An answer does not establish scientific resolution or authorize a run.

An unanswered or unknown response is not consent to the recommendation. Continue only justified work within existing authority and budget, naming its provisional team direction and conditions in the phase summary and scientific account. Neither the recommended, most general nor most conservative formulation is an automatic default. A change to a published method uses the existing revision route, not a presentation repair or saved answer alone.

Optional versus waiting-for-input comes from actual run/request state, not severity or prose. Pause only through an existing authorized mechanism when missing input makes the next action unjustified; explain that action and what can proceed. Members propose in existing owned outputs; the Lead consolidates where permitted. This adds no authority or stage. A report-local discussion or editorial task keeps its existing input restrictions. Independent reviewers record open questions in their owned findings and do not gain private researcher dialogue.
